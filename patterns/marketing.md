# Marketing patterns

## Page anatomy

1. Compact navigation with one primary action.
2. One high-impact Degular Display statement.
3. A specific Plus Jakarta Sans explanation and proof.
4. Real product or creator imagery.
5. Feature storytelling in alternating compositions.
6. One closing action and a practical footer.

## Type

- Hero display: `clamp(3.5rem, 9vw, 8rem)`, tight leading and tracking.
- Section display: `clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`.
- Body: 16–22px with short line lengths.
- UI: 14px, 700 weight.

## Geometry

- Navigation and buttons: pills or 12px controls.
- Content cards: 16–24px.
- Section transitions: up to 64px when the shape materially separates chapters.

## Motion

Use motion only to reveal sequence, state change, or relationship. Every animation must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
