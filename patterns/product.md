# Product patterns

## Dashboard anatomy

1. Persistent desktop sidebar or real mobile navigation.
2. Sticky top navigation for workspace context and global actions.
3. One- or two-column operational canvas depending on available width.
4. Cards grouped by user job: setup, usage, growth, learning, management.
5. Dialogs and filters that complete the workflow.

## Type

- Page title: 24–32px, 700–800 weight.
- Card title: 14–18px, 600–700 weight.
- Body/UI: 12–14px, 500–600 weight.
- Metrics: 20–32px with tabular numerals.

## Geometry

- Outer dashboard card: 24px.
- Nested panels: 18px.
- Dense modules: 6–8px.
- Actions: 10–14px or pill when the production component calls for it.

## Data and state

- Show filled bars, areas, segments, or progress rings.
- Preserve zero as zero and label empty states plainly.
- Teal signals positive outcomes; orange signals negative movement; purple is interaction/selection.
- Stack multi-column cards below 1200px when their content becomes cramped.
