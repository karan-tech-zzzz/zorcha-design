Safety

Is Instagram DM Automation Safe?

Automation is safest when it runs through official Meta APIs, respects Instagram’s permissions, and avoids spam-like behavior. Zorcha states that it is a Meta Tech Provider and that its Instagram automations use official Meta APIs.

Meta API information

Comment-to-DM flows rely on Instagram professional account access, approved permissions, and webhook-triggered events. Follow-up messages depend on the user’s response window and Meta’s platform rules.

Safe usage

Use relevant triggers, do not overload followers with repetitive messages, keep opt-in intent clear, and monitor broken permissions from account settings.
