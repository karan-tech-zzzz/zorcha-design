FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zorcha really free for Instagram DM automation?

Yes. Zorcha’s current public positioning includes free Instagram DM automation with unlimited DMs and contacts. Advanced controls sit in paid plans.

Do I need a Business or Creator Instagram account?

Yes. Comment-to-DM automation uses Meta’s official Instagram API, which requires an Instagram professional account connection.

Can Zorcha send a DM when someone comments on a reel?

Yes. Zorcha supports comment-to-DM flows for posts and reels, so creators can turn comments into private conversations.

Can I automate story replies?

Yes. Story replies can be routed into useful automated responses depending on the trigger and account permissions.

Will automation get my account banned?

No tool can bypass Meta rules. Zorcha’s safety position is to use official APIs and help creators stay inside Meta’s requirements, while creators avoid spam-like behavior.
